The Problem

Small Farmers Lack Unified Decision Support

86% of Indian farmers cultivate operational holdings under 2 hectares, suffering severe market and agronomic information asymmetry.

→
Fragmented Agricultural Information
Market prices, soil test cards, weather forecasts, and agronomy advice are scattered across 8+ disconnected government portals.
→
Difficult Multi-Mandi Price Arbitrage
Farmers cannot readily compare APMC modal rates across nearby districts to evaluate true net earnings after transport costs.
→
No Harvest Realization Estimation
Growers are forced to accept middlemen quotes without knowing their true farm-gate value, loading deductions, and mandi cess.
→
Delayed Crop Disease Identification
20% to 35% preventable yield loss occurs annually because farmers lack macroscopic field diagnostic tools and expert extension access.
→
Language & Telephony Accessibility Barriers
Complex government agricultural datasets are rarely synthesized into regional vernaculars or accessible via feature-phone telephony.

User Journey

From Seed to Sale — The Farmer's Workflow

A seamless 6-step journey designed for intuitive field and mandi decision making.

01
Select Crop & District
Farmer selects crop & location, or taps "Use My GPS" for instant auto-centroid locking.
02
Compare Nearby Mandis
Compares prices across 3 neighboring mandis, deducting logistics to calculate true in-hand cash.
03
Sculpt Field Boundary
Inspects Sentinel-2 NDVI health, drags polygon vertices on satellite map, and verifies parcel acreage.
04
Receive AI Advisory
Gemini 2.0 generates a seasonal calendar, organic nutrient schedule, and soil carbon rating.
05
Diagnose Crop Disease
Snaps a photo of an infected leaf; Gemini Vision returns organic remedies and exact chemical dosage.
06
Execute & Verify Sale
Follows the 11-step Mandi Selling Checklist, checks electronic weighbridge, and saves trade slips.
